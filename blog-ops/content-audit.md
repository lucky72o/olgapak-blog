## Observations & opportunities

- **Cluster balance:** Productivity & Planning (7) and the combined AI + Reviews (8) are the two heaviest areas. Note-Taking (4) and Students/EdTech (4) are solid secondary pillars. Blogging (1) and Humor (1) are one-off outliers.
- **Internal-linking structure is strong within AI:** the "Best AI Note Taker Apps" and "Best AI Scheduling Assistant Apps" round-ups naturally feed the Fathom, Motion, and Sunsama single-product reviews. The "Sunsama vs Motion" comparison closes that loop.
- **Aging content:** Everything except the planning-tips post (updated May 2025) still carries "2023" or "2024" in the title. The tool reviews (Motion, Sunsama, Fathom) and student AI posts are the strongest candidates for a refresh — they're commercial-intent and now ~2+ years stale.
- **Thin clusters to grow or prune:** Blogging and Humor each have a single post. If blogging is a strategic monetization angle, it needs supporting posts; otherwise it's an orphan. The meme post is fine as an engagement/link asset.
- **Site taxonomy (from sitemap):** the live categories are `productivity`, `artificial-intelligence` (+ `ai-assistants` sub-category), `edtech`, and `humor` — which broadly maps to the clusters above.
