## 1. Strategy in a nutshell

Your last-3-months ranking data shows momentum concentrated in **note-taking** (4 of 8 top posts), **phone/digital-wellbeing** (2), **planning** (1), and your new **free AI tools** section (1). This calendar pours firepower into exactly those clusters instead of chasing new topics.

**The model: hub-and-spoke + money posts.**
- **Spokes (informational):** high-traffic how-to/listicle posts that answer a search query and build topical authority. Their job is rankings + internal links.
- **Hubs / money posts (commercial):** "best X for Y (2026)" round-ups and single-product reviews stuffed with Amazon affiliate links. Spokes link *up* into these.
- Every money post is surrounded by 2–4 supporting spokes so it inherits topical authority and internal PageRank before it needs to rank on its own.

**Why note-taking is the anchor:** it's both your strongest-ranking cluster *and* the best physical-product fit on Amazon (notebooks, pens, highlighters, iPads, desk gear). That alignment is rare — lean into it hard.

**Seasonality:** the commercial student-gear posts (best notebooks, pens, highlighters, iPad notes, AI study tools, gifts for students) are scheduled **August → early September** to intercept back-to-school shopping intent.

## 4. Monetization & internal-linking playbook

**Amazon Associates setup**
- Every commercial post needs the **FTC + Amazon affiliate disclosure** above the fold ("As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases…").
- Use comparison tables with a clear "Best for…" column, a CTA button per product, and a "Top pick / Budget pick / Premium pick" framing — this lifts click-through vs. plain links.
- Prices change; use Amazon's product-link API or "check price on Amazon" CTAs rather than hard-coded prices to stay compliant and evergreen.
- Add a re-usable "You might also need…" product block at the end of *informational* posts to capture affiliate clicks from high-traffic spokes, not just money posts.

**Internal-linking rules (the engine)**
1. Every **spoke links up** to at least one **money post** in its cluster (e.g., every note-taking method post → "Best Notebooks").
2. Every **money post links sideways** to 2–3 related money posts (notebooks ↔ pens ↔ highlighters) to spread affiliate exposure and PageRank.
3. New posts link **back to existing top-8 rankers**, and — where natural — edit those existing rankers to add a link **forward** to the new money post. Your current winners (`aesthetic-notes`, `things-to-do-instead-of-being-on-your-phone`, `outlining-note-taking-method`) are your most valuable link sources — use them.
4. The Cluster-A comparison hub (#4) and wellbeing hub (#12) are pillar pages: link every spoke to its hub and the hub to every spoke.

**Free AI tools flywheel:** posts #10, #18, #36 exist partly to push traffic into your interactive `/ai-tools/` pages (one is already ranking). Those tool pages build email list / brand and can carry their own affiliate placements.
