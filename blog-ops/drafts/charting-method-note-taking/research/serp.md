## Avoid pitfalls

- The 3-step generic breakdown ("identify categories → set up columns before class → record info as it comes") is repeated near-verbatim across Sheridan, York, UTC, Evernote, and Plaud — restating it without adding real texture (timing, a full worked example, common mistakes) would be commoditized and add nothing.
- Ending on a note-taking-app pitch (Evernote, Plaud, Jamie all do this) is explicitly forbidden by the brief and already the dominant failure mode of the SaaS-authored half of this SERP — avoid entirely.
- Unqualified "improves memory/retention" language (York's "Enhanced memorisation," Evernote's "supports better memorization," Jamie's "improves your ability to recall") appears with no citation across three separate sources — the brief already flags this as a trap; repeating it without evidence would just add a fourth uncited voice to the same problem.
