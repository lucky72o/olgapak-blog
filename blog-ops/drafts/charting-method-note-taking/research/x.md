## Angle opportunities for this blog

Patterns that suggest angles or hooks this blog's post should adopt.

- The consistent framing of charting as a second-pass/review method paired with Outline notes (DrAminaYonis) supports positioning charting honestly as often a post-lecture organizing step rather than the primary live-capture method, which lines up with the brief's own "review by covering a column and recalling" step.
- The visible engagement gap between charting and Cornell/Mind Mapping within the same threads (both DrAminaYonis's and TheStrugglingS4's) is more evidence for the "charting is the forgotten method" honest framing this post can lead with, rather than manufacturing false popularity.
- The one real-world example (nursing student's digital charting notes) supports keeping the "when it works" section grounded in genuinely comparable/attribute-heavy content (nursing/medical conditions, drug comparisons) rather than invented examples.
- @Cruxador's format-vs-comprehension critique is a useful honest note for the "common beginner mistakes" section: filling in a chart correctly is not the same as understanding the material, echoing the same "capture vs. learning" theme found independently on Reddit.
