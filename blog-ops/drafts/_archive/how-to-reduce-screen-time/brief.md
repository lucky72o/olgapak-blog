## What the human wants to convey

- PILLAR guide: the complete, practical answer to "how to reduce screen time" —
  broad enough to be the hub the blog's other digital-wellbeing posts point at.
- Reduce screen time without moralizing. The reader already knows their screen
  time is too high; they want mechanics that survive a real week, not shame.
- Distinguish *intentional* screen time (work, study, staying in touch) from the
  scrolling that leaks the day away. The goal is fewer wasted hours, not zero
  screens.
- Give tactics the reader can start the same day (blog.md mission: "hand the
  reader a practical tool or tactic they can use the same day"), ordered from
  lowest-friction to hardest.
- Cover the phone-specific mechanics (Screen Time / Digital Wellbeing settings,
  notifications, home-screen layout, grayscale, app limits, charging the phone
  outside the bedroom) AND the environment/habit layer that makes them stick.
- Be honest that app limits and screen-time timers are trivially dismissed —
  the post should say what to do when willpower alone fails.
- Anchor at least one section in Olga's own experience (timeboxing, beating
  Parkinson's Law) rather than generic advice.
