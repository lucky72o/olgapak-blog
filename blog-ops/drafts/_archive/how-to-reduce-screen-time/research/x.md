### 1. @aviralbhat, first-person quantified list of interventions that got screen time under 90 minutes
- URL: https://x.com/aviralbhat/status/1795770942477451588
- Author: Aviral Bhatnagar (@aviralbhat) | Author bio: not captured in raw data
- Posted: date not captured in raw data
- Likes: 178 | Reposts: 9 | Replies: 9
- Why selected: first-person, quantified (<90 mins), and tactic-level — the single most useful post in the set for concrete tactics.

#### Post text (verbatim)
> I've been trying to reduce screen time and have got down to <90 mins, some interventions:
>
> - No notifications on the home screen except calls / or messages from family - if it is important people will call
> - Keep all "doom-scrolling" apps off your phone - e.g. Twitter/Instagram; I also don't have an Insta account
> - Books for planes, audio content for cars - ensure you do not need to look at the phone
> - No work on the phone, as much on the laptop as possible - email replies, reading PDFs, etc. Ensures you don't go into random time-wasting walks
> - Try picking up the phone only in the first few minutes of every hour (e.g. 1 PM to 1:05 PM/2 PM to 2:05 PM)
> - Keep the phone more than arm's length for when you're working (and not near you on a side table when you're sleeping)
> - Exit as many groups as possible on WhatsApp
> - Track your time (iPhones have an inbuilt screen time)

- The settings checklist in @quotesdaily100's post (grayscale, notifications off, DND after a set hour, app limits, home screen with no social apps) is essentially a crowd-validated version of the brief's required "phone-specific mechanics" section — useful as a sanity check that the brief's planned coverage matches what the audience already shares/wants, not as a source to quote wholesale (it's an aggregator account, not an original voice).
