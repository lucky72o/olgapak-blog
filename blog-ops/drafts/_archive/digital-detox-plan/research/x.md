## Angle opportunities for this blog

- Use the framing that detox culture itself has become a trend/anti-trend pattern (connectivity → detox, hustle → slow living) as light color for why this topic feels urgent right now — but attribute it explicitly as one commentator's read, and keep the post's own voice out of hype/market-opportunity framing, since the brief rules out overhyped claims.
- Position the post's 7-day plan as the realistic middle ground between "do nothing" and the extreme end of the spectrum shown here (a 2-year no-smartphone ranch program) — most readers want neither of those; a week-long ramp with allowances is the credible, attainable format X's own extremes make look approachable by contrast.
- The "fat cat forces a break" app is a good illustration that low-friction, almost playful interruption tools can work as well as (or be more relatable than) expensive retreats — supports the post's likely inclusion of small, low-cost daily tactics rather than positioning paid retreats as necessary.
