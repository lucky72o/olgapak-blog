request_revisions

## Revision instruction (verbatim, for the editor)

In `plan.md`, make these four changes:

1. Under `## Recommended title`, replace the recommended title `51 Screen-Free Hobbies Worth Starting (Most Are Free)` with candidate 1, `51 Screen-Free Hobbies to Try When Scrolling Gets Old`, as the new recommendation. Rewrite the rationale paragraph to state that a per-entry cost-tier check of the planned 51-item list (§Key sections) found roughly 20 of 51 entries (about 39%) genuinely free with no required purchase, concentrated in Section 2, while Sections 3, 4, 5, and 6 are dominated by items needing a real purchase (crochet, embroidery, knitting, plastic model kits, LEGO, painting, cycling, climbing, an instrument, woodwork, baking, bread, fermenting and pickling, houseplants, beekeeping), so "(Most Are Free)" does not clear a strict reading of its own claim and repeats the exact free-vs-actually-not-free blur the post argues against. Remove the two "conditions attach to this recommendation" paragraphs (they were written for candidate 2) and replace with a one-line note that candidate 1 was chosen specifically because it makes no checkable cost claim.

2. Under `## Length target`, rewrite the reasoning paragraph to lead with: "the two competitors closest in item count to this post's planned 51 items are Guardian (55 items, 3,628 words) and Game Quitters (50 items, 3,292 words), averaging ~3,460 words, which supports a 3,000-3,600 target more directly than a blended average across very differently sized lists." Keep the existing bottom-up math (51 entries x ~32 words + framing sections =~3,300) as secondary support. Relabel the "five substantive competitors average roughly 2,700 words" sentence as a size-mismatched reference point, not the primary justification.

3. Under `## Key sections`, in the description for section 9 ("When money, space, or time is the real problem"), add: "The space sub-topic stays concrete and actionable (compact or foldable hobbies already on this list, library or community-space alternatives) and makes no statistic or prevalence claim about how common the space constraint is, since nothing in `facts.md` or `research/*.md` supports one."

4. Under `## External links planned`, add a "Where in the post" column to the table (leave cells blank for Phase 2 to fill) and add this line after the table: "If the Harvard Health row and the `[EXTERNAL_LINK_NEEDED:]` marker both fail to resolve or apply at Stage 3d, the writer finds a substitute external link rather than shipping with fewer than 3, per `blog-craft.md` §External linking."
