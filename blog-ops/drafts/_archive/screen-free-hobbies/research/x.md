## Industry-take themes

- **Theme: the topic is being reported as a generational trend ("Gen Z analog hobby boom"), not just discussed as self-help.**
  - Evidence: "Birding is joining a Gen Z analog hobby boom as more young people are trying to go offline after the screen exhaustion of the pandemic.", @FortuneMagazine, source: https://x.com/FortuneMagazine/status/2093035873877024871
  - **Unverified, resolution required**: this is a tweet, not the underlying article. Per `_x_selection.md` and brief.md, do not cite as fact until fortune.com's actual article is fetched and confirmed to say the same thing.

- **Theme: "analog hobby" is the live vernacular for this concept, more common in organic (non-publisher) posts than "screen-free hobby."**
  - Evidence: "My major analog hobby is gardening", @longislandviper, source: https://x.com/longislandviper/status/2071335291235737795
  - Evidence: "It was designed as a small-scale, analog hobby that uses IMAGINATION", @Cthulhu_Coffee, source: https://x.com/Cthulhu_Coffee/status/2022622816701763891
  - This matches theeverygirl.com's SERP-rank-5 usage of "analog hobbies" as its primary term (see `research/serp.md`).

- **Theme: organic posts are evidence-of-doing (photos/results of the hobby), not advice-giving; nobody organically posts "here are N ideas."**
  - Evidence: the gardening post shows specific plants and outcomes rather than general encouragement, @longislandviper, source: https://x.com/longislandviper/status/2071335291235737795
  - Evidence: the first-person mental-health post names a specific practice ("cozy, screen-free hobbies") tied to an outcome, not a list, @MadisonCummins0, source: https://x.com/MadisonCummins0/status/2040080419148747115
