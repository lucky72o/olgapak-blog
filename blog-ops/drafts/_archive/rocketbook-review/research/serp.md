## Audience inferences

- Primary audience: students and early-career note-takers evaluating a reusable smart notebook (per brief); the rank-1 thread OP asks which model is "best for a college student" (https://www.reddit.com/r/notebooks/comments/xj2z3h/rocketbook_a_gimmick_or_something_useful/)
- Secondary audience: office/meeting note-takers and planners (TrustedReviews, Tech for Travel, Gadgeteer family planner use)
- Reader knowledge level: beginner to intermediate (knows the concept, unsure if it lives up to it)
- Reader intent: commercial

## Angle opportunities

- Whole-lineup verdict in one post: every ranking outlet reviews a single model (Pro, Core, Flex, Everlast, Fusion); none says which model to pick for which reader.
- Freshness: 5 of 6 deep-fetched reviews are 2018-2024; only the Gadgeteer Flex review (Nov 2025) is from the last 12 months. No title carries a year.
- Long-term reality: outlet reviews ran for weeks to a month (Tech for Travel "several weeks", Pocket-lint one month); none covers ghosting, coating wear or pen burn-rate over semesters, which Reddit covers (see `research/reddit.md`).
- Cost over time: only TrustedReviews gestures at it ("good value compared to having to buy notebooks continually"); no one models pens/refills + page packs vs paper.
- Honest "who should skip": CrackBerry and Tech for Travel read like advocacy; the Reddit rank-1 result shows Google rewards skeptical owner voice.
- Settle the contradictions: eraser use (CrackBerry/Smart Home Solver "don't" vs Gadgeteer/Pocket-lint "use for small edits") and "FriXion only" vs owner-reported alternatives.

## Avoid pitfalls

- Restating the scan-symbols-to-cloud walkthrough and the full destinations list; every result does it.
- Eco "save the trees" framing as the lead; CrackBerry and Pocket-lint already own it, and it does not answer "is it worth it".
- Quoting competitor prices; they range 2023-2025 and conflict (brief says prefer tiers).
