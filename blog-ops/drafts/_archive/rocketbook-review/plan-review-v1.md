Written by: plan-reviewer agent (Stage 1c.5). Read by: blog-editor (applies fixes or advances).
Independent review of `blog-ops/drafts/rocketbook-review/plan.md`. The reviewer did NOT write the plan.

## Verdict

request_revisions

One-line rationale: the angle, structure and intent fit are right, but the lineup table (the main information-gain element) rests on a "US store, verified 2026-10-14" claim that no saved capture supports, and the only store capture contradicts it. The table also promises Rocketbook-sourced page counts that facts.md lacks for three models. Separately, the "real cost" section and the meta description promise pen-cost maths that facts.md cannot back.
