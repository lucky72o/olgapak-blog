## Industry-take themes

- **Theme:** Highlighter brand loyalty crosses into the digital-notes world; the Stabilo Boss and Zebra Mildliner color palettes are popular enough that students recreate them as hex codes for annotation apps rather than switching to arbitrary digital colors.
  - Evidence: "STABILO BOSS HEXACODES," 868 likes on the follow-up explainer reply, https://x.com/CoookyMd/status/1301883776117747718
  - Evidence: "MILDLINER HEX CODES (Goodnotes, Notability, Noteshelf)," 46 likes, https://x.com/CoookyMd/status/1301883776117747718

- **Theme:** "Use your favorite highlighters" is treated as a comfort/motivation lever for studying, not a learning-effectiveness one, consistent with the brief's honest-science framing that the tool itself doesn't drive retention.
