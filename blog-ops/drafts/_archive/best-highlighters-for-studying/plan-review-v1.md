### Open questions: reviewer verdicts

- **Pilot Kire-Na provisional 11th pick:** acceptable to keep at 11 provisionally. It is clearly flagged, gated on a real primary-source check at Stage 3d, and the brief itself allows 9-11 with no padding. Make the fallback literal per issue #2 above.
- **Dunlosky 2013 citation as the hook:** not acceptable as currently written, because the plan documents a fallback for the sentence but not for the angle built on it. The underlying paper is real and well-known (Dunlosky, Rawson, Marsh, Nathan & Willingham 2013, *Psychological Science in the Public Interest*), so outright loss is unlikely, but the plan should not let its single differentiating hook depend on an unconfirmed citation without a documented plan B. Fix per issue #1.
- **Two Stabilo Boss entries sharing a body:** acceptable to keep both, no fold-in needed. They map to genuinely different study jobs (heavy-duty saturation vs. pastel-on-a-trusted-body) and two different SERP competitors independently pick them apart (Executive Pens Direct picks Boss Original; Stationery Nerd's top-3 includes Boss Pastel), so this isn't invented differentiation. Sharpen wording per issue #4, but no structural change or swap-in is required.
