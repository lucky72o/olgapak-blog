## Angle opportunities for this blog

- Reframe "productive morning routine" as "short routine + one protected focus block": the most-liked selected post (Marc Lou, 1.6K) and the Corey Ganim and Temiloluwa takes all converge on it, and it matches Olga's timeboxing story.
- Use the Bezos "puttering" example as a gentle anti-hustle permission slip (cite the underlying Lex Fridman episode, not the summary account, if used).
- Borrow the structure of Miles Deutscher's framework (define the outcome, pick 2 to 3 non-negotiables, design a short routine, write a 10 to 15 minute fallback) as a manual, no-AI exercise. Do not frame it as an AI-prompt workflow, given the standing rule against promoting Olga's AI tools.
- Address the "routines make you less productive" objection head-on in an early section, then show how a short routine avoids that trap.
