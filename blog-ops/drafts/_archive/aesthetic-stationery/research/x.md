## Counterpoints / contrarian takes

- Both usable posts already ARE the counterpoint to a naive "buy the pretty haul" framing, there is no separate pro-haul position represented in the usable X data to counter. If the post's planned angle is "aesthetic stationery is a real, low-effort lever on the habit of showing up," both @ElShai_eg and @Fffffddddkkk1 push back on unexamined buying and staged-perfection framing, aligning with (not contradicting) the brief's "honest trade-offs over hype" instruction, and should be read as reinforcing evidence rather than opposition.
