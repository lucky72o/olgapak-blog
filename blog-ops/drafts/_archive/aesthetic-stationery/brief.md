- Content-plan angle (row 23): "COMMERCIAL roundup: Ultimate Aesthetic
  Stationery Haul (25 must-haves)".
- Aesthetic stationery is not just decoration: pretty supplies that you
  actually want to use are a real, low-effort lever on the habit of showing
  up to your notes/planner at all.
- Honest trade-offs over hype. Say when a pretty thing is impractical
  (bleed-through, smudging, price per page) rather than listing 25 items that
  all "changed everything".
- Organize by use case / category (pens, notebooks, planners, washi tape,
  sticky notes, desk accessories, storage) so a reader can jump to what they
  actually need instead of scrolling a flat list of 25.

  `custom-instructions.md` §Content policy, and carry the affiliate
  disclosure paragraph at the end of the intro.
