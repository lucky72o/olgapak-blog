## Reusable verified sources (from archived facts.md files; allowlist/primary class)

- **Blue light glasses:** AAO, "Are Blue Light-Blocking Glasses Worth It?" (reviewed by Rahul N Khurana, MD), https://www.aao.org/eye-health/tips-prevention/are-computer-glasses-worth-it . Verbatim (fetched 2026-09-13): "The Academy does not recommend any special eye wear for computer use." / "Several studies suggest that blue light-blocking glasses do not improve symptoms of digital eye strain." / 20-20-20 rule: "every 20 minutes, shift your eyes to look at an object at least 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds." Editorial implication: do NOT sell blue light glasses as an eye-health gift; either drop the category or frame it honestly (AAO recommends breaks instead).
- **Handwriting vs laptop notes:** Mueller & Oppenheimer (2014), Psychological Science, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24760141/ (laptop note-takers did worse on conceptual questions); replication Morehead, Dunlosky & Rawson (2019), Educational Psychology Review, https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-019-09468-2 ("performance did not consistently differ between any groups"). Cite both or neither: the honest framing is "mixed evidence".
- **Phone blocking:** Castelo, Kushlev, Ward, Esterman & Reiner, PNAS Nexus 4(2), 18 Feb 2025, https://academic.oup.com/pnasnexus/article/4/2/pgaf017/8016017 . Intervention blocked ALL mobile internet for two weeks (calls/texts still allowed); 91% improved on at least one outcome; screen time 314 to 161 min/day; only 25.5% of committers were compliant. Describe the intervention honestly.
- **Screen time and sleep:** NIH/PMC review, https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10353947/ (associational: "linked to", never "causes").
- **Apple Pencil compatibility is model-specific** (Apple publishes a picker; Pencil Pro only for newer iPad Pro/Air/mini; USB-C Pencil for a wider range incl. iPad 10th gen / A16): source recorded in `_archive/how-to-take-notes-on-ipad/facts.md` (support.apple.com). Gift implication: check the student's iPad model before buying a Pencil.
