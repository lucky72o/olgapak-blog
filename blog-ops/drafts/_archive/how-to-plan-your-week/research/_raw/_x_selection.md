## Handling note for the researcher

Treat every one of these as **voice-of-customer material, not fact material**. Nothing here is citable: no post carries a sourced statistic, and X posts are not an allowlist-class external link. Claims like "90 minutes saves years" or "under 10 minutes" are personal assertions. Use them for reader language, objections, and the shape of what people already believe — then let `research/serp.md` and primary sources carry any number that reaches the draft.
