# SERP selection: ai note taking

Observed intent: **commercial investigation with a strong informational sub-intent** — the live top-9
is dominated by AI note-taker PRODUCT pages (Zoom rank 1, Plaud rank 4, Notta rank 5, NoteGPT rank 6,
Otter rank 8) plus three "which tool should I use" roundups (metaview 2, PCMag 3, Medium 7) and one
explainer (Microsoft 9). Searchers are mostly shopping for a tool, and secondarily asking what AI
note-taking even is and how it works. The SERP skews hard toward MEETING transcription; almost
nothing serves the student/lecture use case, and nothing serves "how do I fit AI into a note-taking
system that still helps me remember things". That gap is this post's opening.

Note for Stage 1c/2: `brief.md` set intent `how_to` from the content-plan angle. The observed intent
above is authoritative and is NOT a pure how-to. The post should carry an explainer + a real
workflow + an honest, use-case-tagged tool section — without becoming the scored roundup that
`ai-study-tools` (content-plan row 18) is reserved for.

Reviewed the top 9 results from `_serp.json`. Selected 5 (target 5–8, hard cap 8; fewer is allowed
when the SERP is mostly product homepages, which this one is):

1. (rank 1) https://www.zoom.com/en/products/ai-assistant/features/ai-note-taking/ , the rank-1 page
   is a product FEATURE page, not an article, but it is the page Google is rewarding for this exact
   query — worth capturing to see what "AI note taking" is defined as at the top of the SERP and
   which meeting-centric jobs the winner emphasises.
2. (rank 2) https://www.metaview.ai/resources/blog/ai-notetaking-apps , a genuine article and the
   highest-ranking one: 12 apps organised BY USE CASE, which is the structural pattern to beat.
3. (rank 3) https://www.pcmag.com/picks/best-ai-tools-taking-notes , hands-on tested roundup from an
   established publisher; the most credible source of accuracy/feature claims in this SERP and the
   best guide to which tools a reader will already have heard of.
4. (rank 7) https://medium.com/@milanpatel01/i-tested-30-ai-note-taking-tools-for-college-heres-what-actually-works-and-what-s-a-complete-6025ee25ad63 ,
   the ONLY result written for the college/lecture use case rather than for meetings. Closest to this
   blog's student audience, and likely the richest source of honest "what failed" detail.
5. (rank 9) https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/business-insights-ideas/resources/ai-summarizer-note-taking-tools ,
   the explainer ("What Is AI Note Taking? How It Works") that owns the informational sub-intent.
   Useful for the definition/how-it-works section and for the plain-language jargon translations
   `audience.md` requires.

Skipped:
- rank 4 (uk.plaud.ai — vendor homepage for a hardware recorder, no article content; also a UK
  storefront URL with a tracking param)
- rank 5 (notta.ai — vendor homepage)
- rank 6 (notegpt.io/ai-note-taker — vendor tool landing page)
- rank 8 (otter.ai — vendor homepage)

Four vendor homepages skipped, so 5 selected instead of the usual 6–8. Every remaining genuine
article in the top 10 was taken.
