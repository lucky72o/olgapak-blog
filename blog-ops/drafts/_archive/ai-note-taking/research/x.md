## Industry-take themes

- **Theme:** A minority but vocal local-first / own-your-data position rejects subscription AI note-taking SaaS in favor of self-hosted tools (Obsidian + an LLM layer) that keep files on-device.
  - Evidence: "Most tools save your notes. This one connects them... on your own machine.", @KanikaBK, source: https://x.com/KanikaBK/status/2084628466499231838
  - Evidence: "No subscription, offline, and no data harvesting!", @DataFreedotAI (reply, self-promotional but reinforces the theme), source: https://x.com/KanikaBK/status/2084628466499231838
- **Theme:** Free, general-purpose AI tools (Google NotebookLM/Gemini) are being positioned as good-enough substitutes for paid dedicated note-taking apps, particularly for source-grounded Q&A and citation-backed summaries.
  - Evidence: "Google's AI note-taking app just got a major upgrade with Gemini. And it's 100% free to use.", @heysajib, source: https://x.com/heysajib/status/1734906195109183649
  - Evidence: "The AI will answer your questions using only the files you uploaded. It will not guess or make things up...", @Zenith_coder, source: https://x.com/Zenith_coder/status/2095430938801123590
- **Theme:** There's real skepticism/fatigue toward the flood of near-identical "AI note-taking app" launches, alongside a persistent minority preference for plain paper.
  - Evidence: "probably another one of those vibe coded AI note-taking apps. I've seen at least a dozen of these on my tl today and they're all the same.", @wojakcodes, source: https://x.com/wojakcodes/status/1968572197296877869
  - Evidence: "Paper note superiority.", @NateLorenzen (reply), source: https://x.com/wojakcodes/status/1968572197296877869
