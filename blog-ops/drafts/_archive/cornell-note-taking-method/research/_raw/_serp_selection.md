Observed intent: informational. The live top results are educational how-it-works guides — Cornell University's own Learning Strategies Center, university library guides (York), Wikipedia, and note-app blogs — explaining what the method is, how to set up the page, when to use it, often with a template. Confirms the informational_pillar plan.

5. (rank 7) https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_Notes — history/origin (Walter Pauk, 1950s), canonical structure, and cites primary academic sources we can harvest as external links.
6. (rank 8) https://www.cultivatedmanagement.com/the-cornell-note-taking-method/ — professional/knowledge-worker angle ("why it still works"), broadens beyond students to our secondary audience.
