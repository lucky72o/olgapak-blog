## Specific issues (only if not `approve`)

None blocking. Two minor, non-blocking notes for the outline stage (verdict remains approve):

- **Minor 1 — first H2 convention (plan §"Key sections").** blog-craft §"Heading structure" says a transactional post's first H2 should frame the list as a question containing the target keyword (e.g., `What are the best notebooks for note-taking?`). The current first H2 is the primer ("How to choose a notebook"). Not a plan-blocker, but the outline should either lead with a keyword-question H2 or fold the keyword-question framing into the primer/table heading.
- **Minor 2 — item count vs word budget (plan §"Length target" + §"products planned").** 15 picks inside 2,400–2,900 words leaves roughly 85–115 words per pick after intro, primer, table, decision recap, and FAQ — tight for the brief's required per-notebook pros/cons + "best for" label. At outline, either target the upper bound of the range (~2,800–3,000) or confirm each pick still earns a genuine trade-off line; if depth suffers, trimming toward ~12 picks is the cleaner lever.
