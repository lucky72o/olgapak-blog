## Angle

Everywhere the Pomodoro Technique gets promoted it sounds frictionless, and everywhere people actually use it they are arguing about it. On X, 45 posts and their replies produced not one genuine criticism; on Reddit, for the identical keyword, the two biggest threads are titled some version of "I actually hate this". This post takes the second group seriously: it teaches the technique properly, says plainly what the evidence does and does not support, and then spends real space on the specific moments the 25/5 rule breaks (deep technical work, hyperfocus, a five-minute break that is really a pee break) and exactly what to change when it does. The promise is not that Pomodoro will fix your focus. It is that you will know within a week whether it fits you, and what to run instead if it doesn't.

## Facts / data to feature prominently

- The 2025 scoping review's real shape: 32 studies, 5,270 participants, only 3 randomised trials, certainty rated moderate to low by its own authors, with publication bias flagged.
- Cirillo tested roughly 10-minute intervals (too short) and hour-long unbroken stretches (too long) before landing on 25, so the number was arrived at by experiment, which is the permission slip to run your own.
- The 2011 Illinois finding that attention is not a tank that empties: performance drops because you stop noticing the task, and a brief break restores it.
- The three operating rules most guides drop: split anything over four pomodoros, batch the tiny tasks, and let a started pomodoro run to the bell.
- The platform contrast: zero criticism found across 45 X posts, versus two "I hate this" threads at the top of Reddit for the same keyword.

## Tone hooks

- Never "this one trick changes everything". The post's credibility rests on saying who it fails for, so the honest sections must not be softened into a token drawbacks list.
- Take the Reddit critics seriously and quote them by subreddit, not username. They are the reason this post exists, not a foil to knock down.
- The evidence hedge is a feature, not a disclaimer to bury. "Mostly positive but low-certainty" said plainly beats "studies show" said confidently.
- Translate on first use: Parkinson's Law, deep work, flow state, timeboxing.
- No prices anywhere: not for Olga's tools (there are none) and not for the Amazon timers (they drift).
- The affiliate disclosure is the last paragraph of the intro, verbatim per `custom-instructions.md`.
