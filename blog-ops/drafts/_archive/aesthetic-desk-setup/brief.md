## What the human wants to convey

Taken from the content-plan row ("COMMERCIAL guide: How to Make Your Desk Aesthetic (setup
ideas & products)") plus this blog's standing positioning. Not a human conversation, see
assumptions.

- An aesthetic desk is not a shopping list, it is a small number of decisions: what stays on
  the surface, where the light comes from, how the cables are handled, and one or two things
  that are yours. Give the reader those decisions first, then the products that serve them.
- Make it achievable on a real budget and in a real room, including rented rooms, dorm desks
  and desks that share a bedroom.
- Tie the look back to focus: a clear surface and a phone that lives somewhere else is the
  part that actually changes how the day goes. This is the blog's own territory (see the
  doomscrolling and screen-time posts) and is what makes this more than a mood board.
- Every product recommendation is a specific, buyable item with a real link, not a category.
