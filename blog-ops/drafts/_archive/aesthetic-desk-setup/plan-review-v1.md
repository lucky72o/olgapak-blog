## Revision instruction (verbatim, for the editor)

In `plan.md`, make two changes before advancing to outline:

1. Under "## Key sections (preview, becomes the outline in Phase 2)", after item 1 ("Why your desk doesn't look like the one you saved"), add: "The intro ends with the standing affiliate disclosure as its last paragraph, in italics, immediately before the first `##` heading: *Some links in this guide are affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate, I earn from qualifying purchases, at no extra cost to you.* Mandatory because this post carries tagged Amazon links, per `custom-instructions.md` §Content policy and `site-conventions.md` §Post furniture."

2. Under "## Facts / data to feature prominently", add a final bullet: "Step 6 ('Give your phone somewhere else to live') has no dedicated fact, stat, or quote in `facts.md`. It runs on reasoned advice plus the `how-to-stop-doomscrolling` and `how-to-reduce-screen-time` internal links only. Do not invent a phone-usage or attention-span statistic to support it; if a stat is wanted, mark it `[VERIFY:]` per `blog-craft.md`."

(Issue 3 is a minor, non-blocking suggestion: consider adding an outline-stage note that multi-product steps name one primary pick per decision with any alternate as a brief aside, to keep faith with the plan's own "not a shopping list" angle.)
