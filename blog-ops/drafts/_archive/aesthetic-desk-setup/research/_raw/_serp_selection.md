# SERP selection: aesthetic desk setup

Observed intent: **informational-visual with a strong commercial tail** — the live top results
are idea/inspiration galleries ("20 aesthetic desk setup ideas", "10 ideas to transform your
desk") published mostly by e-commerce blogs that end in a shop-this-look list, plus Pinterest
boards, a Reddit community, Instagram/TikTok reels and a YouTube desk tour. Google is NOT
serving a buying-guide comparison; it is serving *ideas first, products second*. There is no
AI Overview and no featured snippet, but there is a People-Also-Ask block whose first question
is literally "How do I make my desk look aesthetic?" — i.e. the searcher wants a method, and
then wants to know what to buy.

Reviewed 16 results (page 1 organic ranks 1–7 as captured, plus page 2 recorded as ranks
11–19, because page 1 held only four genuine articles once Pinterest boards, a subreddit and a
homepage were removed). Selected 7 for deep fetch:

1. (rank 3) https://www.aestheticroomcore.com/blogs/aesthetic-room-blog/20-aesthetic-desk-setup-ideas-for-work-study-plus-where-to-shop — the closest direct competitor: ideas list plus an explicit "where to shop" section, which is exactly the ideas+products shape our own post takes.
2. (rank 4) https://slouchonline.com/aesthetic-desk-setup-ideas/ — a high-ranking ideas listicle; tells us the section count and heading language Google is currently rewarding for this exact phrase.
3. (rank 6) https://orderofficefurniture.co.uk/blogs/order-office-furniture/aesthetic-desk-setup-ideas-for-2025 — a furniture retailer's take with a year-stamped angle; useful for seeing which "trend" framings appear and how thin the product advice actually is.
4. (rank 11) https://stylebyemilyhenderson.com/how-to-style-your-desk — the only established interior-design authority in the set, and the only result whose promise is "cute AND functional". Best source of genuine styling principles rather than a shopping list.
5. (rank 14) https://www.savespendsplurge.com/functional-essentials-of-an-aesthetic-and-minimal-cozy-cosy-desk-setup-when-you-are-wfh-part-1/ — leads with function ("functional essentials") for a work-from-home desk; the counterweight to the pure-aesthetics results and closest to this blog's productivity angle.
6. (rank 15) https://nikkilo.com/blog/aesthetic-desk — a first-person cozy-minimalist desk tour with named items; the best source for how specific, real product picks get written up credibly.
7. (rank 17) https://octet.design/journal/aesthetic-desk-setup-ideas/ — a design studio's editorial take, explicitly framed against "your Pinterest desk"; a different vocabulary from the retailer blogs and a check on whether any design principles are being stated that the listicles miss.

Skipped:
- rank 1, 7, 13 (Pinterest boards — image collections, no article body to analyse; their presence IS the intent signal and is recorded in `_serp.json`)
- rank 2 (minimaldesksetups.com — site homepage / gallery, not an article)
- rank 5 (reddit.com/r/desksetup — a subreddit landing page; Reddit is covered properly by Stage 1.5a, which fetches real threads)
- rank 12 (everlastingfabric.com — a fabric shop's blog covering the same "ideas for work & study" angle already captured by rank 3; adds no new angle)
- rank 16, 19 (TikTok / YouTube video pages — no extractable article body)
- rank 18 (amazon.com search results page — a product listing, not an article)
