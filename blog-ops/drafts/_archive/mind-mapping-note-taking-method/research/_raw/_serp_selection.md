# SERP selection: mind mapping note taking

Observed intent: informational / how-to. The live top results are dominated by university study-skills guides (York, Open University, Hull) explaining how to mind-map for notes, one linear-vs-mind-map comparison (Evernote), a "methods" listicle (Goodnotes), and mind-mapping tool pages (Mindmup, Canva). SERP feature: People Also Ask (no AI overview, no featured snippet captured). This is a "teach me the method + when to use it" query, not commercial.

Reviewed top 9 results from `_serp.json`. Selected 5 (target 5–8; fewer is fine here — the tool homepages and the duplicate fragment URL add no new angle):

1. (rank 1) https://subjectguides.york.ac.uk/note-taking/mind-map — University of York study guide; high-authority, exactly on-intent (how to build a mind map for note-taking). Anchor source for the how-to.
2. (rank 2) https://help.open.ac.uk/notetaking-techniques/mind-maps — Open University help centre; the "basic principle" + practical steps. (rank 4 is this same URL with a text-fragment anchor — deduped.)
3. (rank 5) https://evernote.com/learn/linear-notes-vs-mind-maps-which-is-better-for-note-taking — linear notes vs mind maps; the source for the "when mind mapping beats linear notes (and when it doesn't)" section. Fragment stripped.
4. (rank 6) https://www.goodnotes.com/blog/mind-mapping-methods — "Top 4 Mind Mapping Methods for Note-Taking"; covers method variations/structure — useful for organizing the how-to and the digital angle.
5. (rank 8) https://libguides.hull.ac.uk/digitalnotetaking/mindmap — University of Hull libguide; digital note-taking + mind-mapping software angle without being a product page.

Skipped:
- rank 3 (Mindmup) — pure tool homepage, no how-to content.
- rank 4 — duplicate of rank 2 (text-fragment anchor of the same Open University page).
- rank 7 (Canva mind map maker) — product/landing page; university guides already cover the how-to more credibly.
- rank 9 (r/PKMS "Mind map + note taking?") — a Reddit thread; covered by the dedicated Reddit research stage (1.5a), not fetched here as a SERP page.
