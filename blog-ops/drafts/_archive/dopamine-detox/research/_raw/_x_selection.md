# X selection: dopamine detox

Tab used: **Top** (`f=top`). Not re-pulled as `f=live`: the Top tab was
genuinely on-topic (roughly 26 of 30 captured posts are about dopamine detox
itself), so the relevance problem the fallback exists for did not occur. Three
of the four off-topic hits are the same JSON-prompting thread, matched because
the phrase "dopamine detox" appears inside a worked example.

**The single most useful finding is the shape of the corpus, not any one post.**
X's dopamine-detox discourse is overwhelmingly hustle-culture self-improvement:
"7 steps to detox your dopamine levels and reset your mind", "reset your brain
for success in 7 days (backed by science)", "9 lessons from the book Dopamine
Detox", the same listicle cross-posted by several accounts (ranks 5, 12, 17 are
near-identical). That is exactly the register `blog-ops/profile/audience.md`
§Language to avoid bans and `brief.md` §Avoid list rules out. It is also the
register a reader has already been marinated in before they land on our post,
which is a reason to open by naming it.

Selected 5, chosen for perspective spread rather than for engagement alone:

1. (rank 16) https://x.com/eptwts/status/1954260984266211460 , @eptwts, 917 likes / 40 replies, 2025-08-09.
   The sharpest counter-take in the set: successful people get flooded with
   dopamine *by working*. This is the debunk in the audience's own vernacular,
   and the reply thread is where the argument actually gets tested.
2. (rank 27) https://x.com/thedankoe/status/1857074770057089172 , @thedankoe, 1,529 likes / 202 replies, 2024-11-14.
   Highest reply volume of anything on-topic. "Going on a walk is a micro
   dopamine detox" is the moderate reframe, and the disagreement underneath it
   is the useful part.
3. (rank 3) https://x.com/gregisenberg/status/1736058260908572793 , @gregisenberg, 914 likes / 89 replies, 2023-12-16.
   Cultural and commercial framing ("someone will make $1B in the digital detox
   space", "people are realizing their phones are drugs"). Explains why the
   term is everywhere, which the post needs in order to be honest about it.
4. (rank 4) https://x.com/simrunshroff/status/1637469264817926144 , @simrunshroff, 119 likes / 45 replies, 2023-03-19.
   The protocol camp, but the rare version with more replies than likes — the
   comments are people arguing about what belongs on the list, not applauding.
   Representative of the "5 ways to do a dopamine detox" genre with its
   objections attached.
5. (rank 7) https://x.com/liamottley_/status/2094070276031914357 , @liamottley_, 429 likes / 14 replies, 2026-08-30.
   The most recent post captured and a first-person trial of the extreme
   version (work, read, stare at the wall). This is precisely the variant
   `brief.md` wants distinguished from the usable one.
