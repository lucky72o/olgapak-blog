## Industry-take themes

- **Theme:** Methods are tool-agnostic, the value is in the structure (cues + summaries), not the paper-vs-app medium.
  - Evidence: "I like having a method built around Cues and Summaries that are independent of the note-taking medium," @TfTHacker, source: https://x.com/TfTHacker/status/1695390088564269139
  - Evidence: "the technique can just as easily be implemented in the digital realm," @TfTHacker, source: https://x.com/TfTHacker/status/1695390088564269139

- **Theme:** "Right tool for the job", pick the method by task, don't crown one winner (mirrors the Reddit "no single method wins" theme).
  - Evidence: "the question isn't which is better, but what is the proper method for the task at hand... I use multiple methods depending on the type of note, type of content, and the end result," @TfTHacker, source: https://x.com/TfTHacker/status/1695390088564269139
