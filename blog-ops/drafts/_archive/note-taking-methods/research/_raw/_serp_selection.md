# SERP selection: note-taking methods

Observed intent: **informational** — the live top results are academic study-skills guides (universities: UTC, UNC, Newcastle, Open University, Oxford Learning) and productivity-app blogs (Goodnotes, Box) that list and compare note-taking methods, plus one Reddit discussion. No transactional/shopping intent. Readers want to understand the methods and which to use — a comparison/decision guide is the right structure.

Reviewed top 8 results from `_serp.json`. Selected 7 for deep fetch (target 5–8):
