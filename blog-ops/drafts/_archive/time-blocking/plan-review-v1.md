## Revision instruction (verbatim, for the editor)

Apply these four changes to `blog-ops/drafts/time-blocking/plan.md`:

1. In `## Recommended title`, replace the title with `Time Blocking: How to Do It (Without It Falling Apart)` (54 chars) so the literal target keyword "time blocking" appears in the title, matching all 7 analyzed SERP competitors per `research/serp.md` §"Title modifier tally". Update the rationale line to note the keyword-presence fix while keeping the collapse/recovery differentiator from the rejected option 3.

2. In `## Key sections`, replace the 8-item content list (current §1–§8) with a 5-item list: (1) What is time blocking, and why it works — merging current §1 and §3; (2) Time blocking vs timeboxing vs task batching vs day theming — unchanged; (3) How to time block your day, step by step — absorbing current §5 ("how much to plan") as a step and current §8 ("tools you need") as a closing beat; (4) Why your time blocks fall apart by 11am (and how to recover) — unchanged content, but see item 3 below for a wording fix; (5) Who time blocking doesn't suit (and what to do instead) — unchanged. Keep CTA and FAQ as the final two entries.

3. In the merged "why blocks fall apart" section, reframe the failure mode as "over-packed calendars with zero buffer between blocks" rather than "clock-precision, per-task scheduling with no slack," and add one sentence explicitly linking this back to the timeboxing/time-blocking distinction drawn earlier in the post (not re-explaining it in new words). Note this fix in the plan so the outline stage carries it forward.

4. In the merged "how to time block your day" section's tools beat (formerly §8), attribute the "keep the system simple, plain calendar over a dedicated app" point specifically to the u/SalkMe quote already in `facts.md`, and do not state a broader "probably just your calendar" claim unless that fuller framing is added to `facts.md` first.
