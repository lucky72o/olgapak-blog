## Industry-take themes

- **Theme:** Over-customizing notes (templates, pen aesthetics) is a self-reported and instructor-observed failure mode, not a hypothetical one, and it can have real consequences (a tablet ban).
  - Evidence: "You buy an iPad for better note-taking ability but then you get disappointed with your handwriting because it doesn't match the aesthetics you had in your head. So now your iPad is in some corner collecting dust", @manthanguptaa, source: https://x.com/manthanguptaa/status/1778019825283215672
  - Evidence: professor banned tablet note-taking because "a past student spent too long making their notes look pretty", @antlerposter, source: https://x.com/antlerposter/status/2089730464873369611

- **Theme:** The iPad's dual identity as both a productivity device and a consumption/entertainment device is a recurring worry, independent of the (unsourced) "85%" statistic.
  - Evidence: "ipad + pencil = productivity. ipad + youtube = doomscrolling", reply from @romaluev on @andres_vidoza's post, source: https://x.com/andres_vidoza/status/1972300779114611060
  - Evidence: "And most owners treat it like an iPhone with a bigger display.", @jamescoder12, source: https://x.com/jamescoder12/status/2087549656813486538 (post argument itself is citable; the 85% figure inside it is not)

- **Theme:** A paper-feel screen protector (Paperlike specifically, matching the SERP's hellobrio recommendation) plus GoodNotes is the most commonly volunteered practical fix for poor handwriting quality on glass.
  - Evidence: "Use - 1. Paperlike screen protector 2. GoodNotes. These two combined have made my experience a lot better.", @ManishPushkar (reply), source: https://x.com/manthanguptaa/status/1778019825283215672
