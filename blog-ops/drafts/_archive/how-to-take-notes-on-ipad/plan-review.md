## Ruling on the Issue 3 deviation

Confirmed, not rejected. Iteration 1's Issue 3 asked for one line stating that H2 3 and the review sub-step "don't require a facts.md citation." The editor instead named the specific facts.md rows that already exist for each: Apple's organizational facts (iCloud sync, first-line-becomes-title, Quick Notes) for H2 3, and handwriting search for the review sub-step. I checked both anchor sets against `facts.md` §"Apple feature facts" directly; every fact named is present verbatim there with a verified status. This is a stricter result than what I asked for: it satisfies the outline template's "every H2 cites >=1 fact" rule on its own terms rather than pre-authorizing two uncited H2s, and it introduces no overclaim risk since none of these facts are being stretched to support a numeric or contested claim, only routine Apple feature behavior. Approved as written.

One implementation note worth carrying to Stage 3b rather than blocking this gate: H2 3's core content is Olga's own notebook/folder methodology, and the Apple facts attached to it (sync, auto-titling, Quick Notes) are organizational-adjacent but not the thing the H2 is actually teaching. The outline/draft stage should weave them in naturally (e.g., auto-titling as a naming-convention aside, iCloud sync as the reason you don't need a manual backup step) rather than force-fitting them as the section's evidentiary backbone. This is a drafting-craft note, not a plan defect.
