## Industry-take themes

- **Theme:** Clinical/professional voices on X dismiss the eye-strain and eye-disease claims outright, while giving qualified ground only on sleep.
  - Evidence: "This is the only potential benefit. Blue light can screw up melatonin levels, so buying these to help you sleep is defensible, OR just don't stare at your phone before bed.", @DGlaucomflecken, source: https://x.com/DGlaucomflecken/status/1285053673580576768
  - Evidence: "So should you buy blue light blocking glasses? I say don't waste your money. The @aao_ophth would agree with me.", @DGlaucomflecken, source: https://x.com/DGlaucomflecken/status/1285053673580576768
  - This mirrors the SERP's Cleveland Clinic / AAO / Cochrane findings almost exactly (see serp.md) — independent corroboration across platforms.

- **Theme:** Ordinary wearers' replies split roughly into "helped with dry eyes/comfort" vs. "no effect," rarely landing on a clean eye-strain verdict either way, and several conflate the glasses with unrelated fixes (Rx correction, artificial tears, screen-distance habits).
  - Evidence: "My eyes were gwtting so dry I didnt even wanna work... I would say it's noticeable for me. Eyes are less dry", @mbcacciano, source: https://x.com/traversymedia/status/1529138541850152960
  - Evidence: "What helped me more than these glasses were eye drops (artificial tears)", @lesrevenants967, source: https://x.com/traversymedia/status/1529138541850152960
  - Frequency: this mixed, self-attribution-uncertain pattern is the dominant tone across the 13 captured replies to the traversymedia thread.

- **Theme:** A live undercurrent of suspicion about eyewear-industry pricing and upselling exists independent of the blue-light question specifically — buyers assume they're being overcharged for lens features.
  - Evidence: "the lens upgrades your optometrist pushes are the highest-margin upsells in the entire transaction — and most of them are available online for 80% less.", @HeyOscarx, source: https://x.com/HeyOscarx/status/2096206167714476236
  - Corroborated independently by Reddit (see reddit.md, r/glasses thread) — two different platforms surfacing the same buyer-protection concern without prompting.
