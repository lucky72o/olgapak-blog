Written by: plan-reviewer agent (Stage 1c.5). Read by: blog-editor (applies fixes or advances).
Independent review of `blog-ops/drafts/best-blue-light-glasses/plan.md`. The reviewer did NOT write the plan.

## Verdict

request_revisions

One-line rationale: the honest-evidence-gate-then-9-picks angle is coherent and genuinely differentiated (it matches how the SERP's two most-earned competitors are structured and adds a buyer-protection angle none of them use), but the plan has four concrete, fixable gaps: no locked-in affiliate disclosure, a length target too tight for the H2/pick set it commits to, one H2 sub-claim with no backing in `facts.md`, and an unflagged top-10-SERP-competitor domain among the planned product links.

## Revision instruction (verbatim, for the editor)

Apply these five edits to `blog-ops/drafts/best-blue-light-glasses/plan.md` before advancing to outline:

1. Add a new line locking in the mandatory disclosure: "Intro's last paragraph, immediately before the first `##`, is the mandatory disclosure: *Some links in this guide are affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate, I earn from qualifying purchases, at no extra cost to you.*"
2. In `## Length target`, change "2,400 to 2,800 words" to "2,800 to 3,300 words" and add: "The wider range is deliberate: 8 H2s plus 9 full picks is structurally more ambitious than any single SERP competitor except Wirecutter (3,920 words for 4 picks); keep the 9 picks in tight bullet format, not full prose, to hit this range without padding."
3. In `## Key sections` item 6, replace "How to buy a pair without getting upsold (the optician-margin reality, online vs in-store, what to say no to)" with "How to buy a pair without getting upsold (the optician-margin reality per `facts.md` Quotes rows 8-9, what to say no to at the counter; do not compare online vs in-store pricing or cite any specific discount percentage — the only source for that claim is rejected in `facts.md` as unsourced)."
4. In `## Open questions`, expand the Zenni/EyeBuyDirect/Warby Parker bullet to add: "Zenni's brand-page fallback (zennioptical.com) ranks #9 in this keyword's primary SERP; its product link must carry `rel=\"sponsored nofollow\"` per the affiliate convention, not a plain link. Felix Gray (rank 1) and GUNNAR (rank 5) are assumed to get tagged Amazon links instead of their own top-10-ranking brand pages — confirm this at Stage 3d marker-resolution rather than defaulting silently to the brand page if Amazon doesn't carry the item."
5. In `## Key sections` item 3, add: "Every pick's 'why it earns a place' must ground in comfort, fit, AR coating, colour distortion, Rx availability, or price band — never in blue-light-blocking efficacy, even implicitly. GUNNAR's framing specifically must not imply the tint reduces eye strain during long sessions; ground it in comfort/tint preference only."
