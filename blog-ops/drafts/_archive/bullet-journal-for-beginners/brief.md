## What the human wants to convey

Taken from the content-plan row ("INFO guide: Bullet Journal for Beginners (setup, ideas &
supplies)") plus this blog's standing positioning. Not a human conversation, see assumptions.

- Bullet journalling is a planning *system*, not an art project. The Instagram spreads are
  what make beginners quit before they start; the original method is deliberately fast.
- Give the reader a genuinely minimal starting setup they can finish in one sitting today:
  index, future log, monthly log, daily log, plus rapid logging with its small set of symbols.
- Be honest about who it does and doesn't suit, and about the time cost of migration.
- Supplies section stays practical and cheap-first: one notebook, one pen, nothing else needed.
  Real product links only (no placeholders) per `custom-instructions.md` §Content policy.
- Connect it to the blog's wider planning cluster (weekly planning, time blocking) rather than
  treating bullet journalling as a standalone hobby.
