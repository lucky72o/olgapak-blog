## Judgement calls (per dispatch instructions)

1. **Length target 2,600-3,000 vs. blog-craft's 3,000-5,000 band.** Sound. The plan doesn't just assert a preference, it grounds the deviation in `blog-craft.md`'s own "match top-5 SERP competitor length" clause plus verified competitor-length math and a verified house-length range. The one flaw is arithmetic, not reasoning: the section budget sums to 3,010, ten words over the plan's own ceiling. Fixable with a one-line trim, not a rethink.

2. **Zero Amazon affiliate links, routed internally instead.** Sound. `custom-instructions.md` §Content policy's affiliate mandate is explicitly scoped to `"best X" / commercial roundups`, and this post is neither by the plan's own angle (an informational guide whose thesis is "buy almost nothing"). Routing to `/best-notebooks-for-note-taking` and `/best-pens-for-note-taking` matches `brief.md`'s own listed internal-link targets for exactly this purpose, and genuinely serves the reader who wants to upgrade without undercutting the post's central argument. No revision needed here.

3. **The ADHD FAQ item, kept with a mechanical, [EXTERNAL_LINK_NEEDED:]-marked answer.** Sound. This matches `facts.md`'s own rejected-claim guidance verbatim (mechanical framing only, no clinical claim, drop or stay experiential if Stage 3d finds nothing), which is the right level of caution for a YMYL-adjacent question this blog has zero clinical authority to answer, while still serving real, documented search demand (PAA). No revision needed here.

4. **The 15.9M-views framing device, used unlinked.** Not sound as currently justified. The number itself is fine to use (it's a verified live-SERP reading of a real, checkable metric, not an invented stat), but "unlinked prose framing" is the wrong resolution because a citable URL for it exists and was simply missed: `research/_raw/_serp.json` `videoCarousel[0].url` = `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fm15cmYU0IM`. This should become a normal external link (primary_source class, same reasoning as `rydercarroll.com`), not a dangling unlinked figure. Leaving it unlinked when a legitimate citation is sitting in the raw research file is the actual soft-fabrication risk here, not the number itself.
