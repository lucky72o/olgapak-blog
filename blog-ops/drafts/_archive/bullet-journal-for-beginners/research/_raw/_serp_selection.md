# SERP selection: bullet journal for beginners

Observed intent: **informational, beginner how-to** — the live page-1 results are almost
entirely "here is the method and here is how to start it" guides (two of them explicitly
badged "ultimate"/"definitive"), fronted by a video carousel led by Ryder Carroll's own
4-minute explainer and backed by a shopping module selling his book and generic planners.
Nobody on page 1 is ranking a product roundup; the commercial layer sits in the shopping
module and in "best bullet journal for beginners" as a *related* search, not in the organic
results themselves. So: an explanatory how-to guide with a numbered setup path, not a
listicle, and not a product roundup.

Two structural signals worth carrying into the plan:

- **Video dominates.** A 4:12 video from 2015 with 15.9m+ views still occupies page 1, and
  three more beginner-setup videos sit alongside it. Readers who land on text want the parts
  video does badly: the exact symbol set they can copy, the "what do I write on page one"
  answer, and an honest word on what to skip.
- **People-also-ask is all beginner anxiety**, not curiosity about the method's theory:
  "What to put at the beginning of a bullet journal?", "How to start a bullet journal step by
  step?", "What are some common mistakes to avoid when bullet journaling?", "Is BuJo good for
  ADHD?". Those four map cleanly onto FAQ slots.

Related searches skew hard toward *supplies and examples*: "bullet journal for beginners
notebook", "best bullet journal for beginners", "bullet journal for beginners template",
"bullet journal ideas for students", "bullet journal examples", "with pictures". The supplies
and ideas dimensions of the content-plan angle are validated by the SERP.

Reviewed 7 organic results (page 1 held only 7, confirmed by scrolling to the pagination
footer; the remaining page-1 real estate was video, shopping and PAA). Selected 6 for deep
fetch:

1. (rank 1) https://www.thelazygeniuscollective.com/blog/how-to-bullet-journal , top-ranked
   "absolute ultimate guide"; sets the length and structure bar we have to match or beat.
2. (rank 2) https://bulletjournal.com/blogs/faq/how-to-start-a-bullet-journal-for-beginners ,
   the **official** source (Ryder Carroll's own site). Primary source for the method's
   definitions, the symbol set, and the four core collections; every definitional claim in our
   post should trace here rather than to a competitor's paraphrase.
3. (rank 4) https://www.hobbycraft.co.uk/ideas/bullet-journaling-for-beginners.html , a craft
   retailer's beginner guide. Different incentive structure to the blogs (it sells the
   supplies), so it shows how the supplies question gets answered when someone is motivated to
   over-answer it. Useful as a foil for our cheap-first supplies stance.
4. (rank 6) https://www.howjoyful.com/bullet-journal-guidelines/ , "The Definitive Guide for
   beginners"; long-form competitor, likely the most comprehensive text result on the page.
5. (rank 7) https://mattragland.com/bullet-journal-beginners , short personal "5 tips" take.
   Deliberately picked against the grain of the long guides: it shows the minimal-practice
   angle, which is close to the angle in our brief.
6. (rank 8) https://medium.com/@phoenixsmith010/bullet-journaling-101-a-guide-for-complete-beginners-e10b409e295c ,
   a recent (9-month-old) 101 framing; useful for how a newer writer sequences the same
   material. May be metered by Medium; if the fetch returns a paywall stub, that is recorded
   and the other five carry the analysis.

Skipped:

- rank 3 https://www.reddit.com/r/bulletjournal/comments/60e15w/faq_for_beginners/ , a Reddit
  thread ranking organically. Not skipped because it is weak (it is the opposite: a beginner
  FAQ pinned by the community), but because Reddit gets its own dedicated deep-fetch stage
  (1.5a) that parses threads properly, comments included. Carried into the Reddit selection
  instead of being fetched twice as flat page text.
- rank 5 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fm15cmYU0IM , video, not an article. Its existence and
  reach are recorded in `_serp.json` as a SERP-shape signal.
