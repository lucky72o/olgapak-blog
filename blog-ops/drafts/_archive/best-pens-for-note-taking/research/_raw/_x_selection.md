## Tab choice + why this source is thin

**Kept: `f=live` (Latest). Discarded: `f=top`.**

- `f=top` for the target keyword returned 7 posts, all from a single startup/AI account, none about pens — engagement-ranked noise with zero topical relation.
- A supplementary high-engagement query (`"best pen" notes OR notetaking OR "note taking" min_faves:20`, `f=top`) was tried to find real takes with traction. It returned only **digital** note-taking discourse (Obsidian's valuation, NotebookLM, OneNote alternatives, Sam Altman's note process) — nothing about physical pens. Discarded, not saved.
- `f=live` is the only tab surfacing actual pen talk, so its 12 results are what `_x_search.json` holds (`tabNote` in the file records this).

**Honest read: X is a weak source for this topic.** Physical-stationery opinion lives on Reddit and in the enthusiast blogs, not on X; "best pen" on X is dominated by note-taking *apps*. The engagement bar in the editor persona (likes >50 OR meaningful replies) is unreachable here — the only on-topic posts sit at 2–3 likes. Rather than pad the selection with off-topic posts that clear the engagement bar, the bar is deliberately relaxed and the selection kept small. X should carry **minor** weight at Stage 1b; Reddit is the voice-of-customer source for this post.

## Selected (2 of 12)

1. (rank 7) https://x.com/beethestalli0n/status/1880506929442210147 — @beethestalli0n, 3 likes. A hands-on ranking of 7 numbered pens from a photo, judged specifically on note-taking: which bleed through, which smudge, which is best "if you don't want any of the qualities listed above," plus a longevity claim. Low engagement, but it is exactly the kind of unvarnished first-hand comparison the post's "honest limitations" sections need, and it independently corroborates the bleed-through theme that dominates the Reddit threads.
2. (rank 6) https://x.com/JoseMatzkin/status/1899480245064155630 — @JoseMatzkin, 2 likes, in-thread reply. Specific fineliner detail (Stabilo Point 88 line, 0.4 mm metal-encased tip, the Colorkilla "ink eraser" variant). Useful as a pointer to a fineliner family the mainstream roundups underweight — treated as a lead to verify against the manufacturer, never as a citable spec on its own.
