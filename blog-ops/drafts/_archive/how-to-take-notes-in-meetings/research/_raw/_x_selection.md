# X selection: how to take notes in meetings

## Tab / query note

Query used: `meeting notes note-taking`, **`f=top`** — that is the pull saved as
`_x_search.json` (8 posts after filtering).

A `f=live` re-pull was run on `"meeting notes" min_faves:30` per the documented off-topic
fallback and was **worse**, not better: the Latest timeline for this phrase is dominated by
"10 AI automation ideas for small businesses" style promo threads where "meeting notes" is
one bullet in a list. The Top tab was kept.

Both tabs share the same distortion, worth flagging to the researcher: X discussion of
meeting notes in 2026 is overwhelmingly **AI-notetaker product marketing**, not practitioner
technique. That is itself a finding (it is the noise the reader is wading through), but it
means X contributes fewer genuine takes here than SERP or Reddit. Only 3 of 8 captured posts
clear the "real take, not a pitch" bar, so 3 were selected rather than the 5 cap.

## Selected (3 of 8)

1. (rank 4) https://x.com/kabirnagral/status/2094652022914203656 , @kabirnagral, 259 likes and **208 replies**. "Why has nobody solved for in-person meeting note-taking? If someone has, I'm ready to buy." The reply tree is the value: hundreds of people describing what they actually do in rooms where a laptop or an AI recorder is not welcome. Best voice-of-customer source on X for this post.
2. (rank 3) https://x.com/DrPengSHEN/status/2096437717320278027 , @DrPengSHEN, 146 likes, 13 replies. "I haven't taken a single meeting note in 3 months... while I was busy writing down what you said 30 seconds ago, I was missing what you're saying right now." A genuine, well-argued counter-position and almost exactly the post's own thesis about transcription crowding out attention. Worth engaging with rather than just agreeing.
3. (rank 1) https://x.com/onlyonepsalm/status/2092309012549169638 , @onlyonepsalm, 216 likes, 13 reposts. Builder post, so treat the product claim as promotion, but the framing it argues for , you keep taking your own notes and AI listens in the background as backup , is the honest middle position between "type everything" and "let the bot do it," and it has real engagement behind it.
