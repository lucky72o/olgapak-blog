# SERP selection: how to take notes in meetings

Observed intent: informational / how-to — the live top results are all instructional "how to
take meeting notes" guides, most of them SaaS content-marketing pieces (Asana, Slack,
Teamwork) offering a method plus a downloadable template, alongside one practitioner essay
and one Reddit discussion. No shopping, no AI Overview on this pull; People Also Ask present.

Reviewed the top 7 captured results from `_serp.json`. Selected 6 (target 5–8, hard cap 8;
fewer allowed when the SERP warrants it) for deep fetch:

1. (rank 1) https://medium.com/envoy-design/how-to-take-good-meeting-notes-bf49702a03c8 , the rank-1 practitioner essay rather than a SaaS blog; shows what an opinionated, experience-led angle looks like at the top of this SERP.
2. (rank 3) https://asana.com/resources/meeting-notes-tips , the canonical "tips + track actions" corporate guide; likely the structural template the SERP rewards (what to capture, action items, tooling).
3. (rank 4) https://smallsheds.garden/blog/2025/five-levels-of-note-taking-in-meetings/ , a genuinely different framing ("five levels"), useful for finding an angle the SaaS pieces don't have.
4. (rank 5) https://duckalignment.academy/meeting-notes/ , independent site with a facilitation/meeting-practice slant; likely covers the running-the-meeting case the tool blogs skip.
5. (rank 6) https://slack.com/blog/productivity/how-to-take-effective-meeting-notes , large-brand guide; good for coverage baseline and for spotting the AI-notetaker framing.
6. (rank 7) https://www.teamwork.com/blog/how-to-take-meeting-notes/ , explicitly template-led ("free template included"), which is the sub-intent the content-plan row names ("templates + tips").
