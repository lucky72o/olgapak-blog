## What the human wants to convey

Taken from the content-plan row ("INFO guide: How to Take Better Notes in Meetings (templates
+ tips)") plus this blog's standing positioning. Not a human conversation, see assumptions.

- Meeting notes fail for a structural reason, not a discipline reason: people try to
  transcribe instead of deciding what matters. The fix is a capture structure chosen before
  the meeting starts, not faster typing.
- Hand the reader a template they can copy into their next meeting today (decisions, action
  items with owner and date, open questions, context), plus the before / during / after
  routine that makes it stick.
- Be honest about the trade-offs: paper vs. laptop, when an AI notetaker earns its place and
  when it makes things worse, and what to do when you are the one running the meeting.
- The five-minute post-meeting pass is the highest-leverage habit in the whole post; make it
  concrete rather than a throwaway line.
