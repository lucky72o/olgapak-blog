# SERP selection: how to stop doomscrolling

Observed intent: informational / problem_solution. The live top results are a Reddit peer-discussion thread (rank 1) plus a stack of health-authority explainers (Mental Health Foundation, Health Assured, Nuffield Health) and practical habit blogs (The Guardian, Dr Gina Cleo, Happiful, Zen Habits). Readers want (a) why the brain does this and (b) concrete tactics to stop — not a product to buy. A numbered "strategies" listicle grounded in the science fits the intent well.

Reviewed top 9 results from `_serp.json`. Selected 7 (target 5–8, hard cap 8) for deep fetch:

1. (rank 2) https://www.mentalhealth.org.uk/explore-mental-health/articles/doomscrolling-tips-healthier-news-consumption — Mental Health Foundation (UK charity), authoritative-allowlist class. Best source for the "why our brains are wired for this" science + healthier-consumption tactics.
2. (rank 3) https://www.healthassured.org/ie/blog/what-is-doomscrolling/ — Health Assured (wellbeing provider). Practical stop/reduce steps + the "when to seek professional help" boundary.
3. (rank 4) https://www.theguardian.com/lifeandstyle/2026/may/22/how-to-quit-doomscrolling-without-quitting-your-phone — The Guardian, authoritative-allowlist. Recent (2026-05), sharp "screentime swaps" framing — quit the habit without quitting the phone.
4. (rank 6) https://www.drginacleo.com/post/how-i-stopped-doom-scrolling-without-going-off-the-grid — Dr Gina Cleo (habit-change researcher), first-person practitioner tactics. Adds a different format/voice and habit-science credibility.
5. (rank 7) https://www.nuffieldhealth.com/article/what-is-doomscrolling-and-how-can-i-stop — Nuffield Health (health provider). Definition + causes + stop tactics from a clinical org.
6. (rank 8) https://happiful.com/tips-to-stop-doomscrolling — Happiful (wellbeing magazine). Compact "6 tips" listicle — shows the competing structure + tactic set to differentiate against.
7. (rank 9) https://zenhabits.net/doomscrolling/ — Zen Habits (Leo Babauta). Mindfulness / urge-surfing angle — the "sit with the urge" and replacement-habit tactics the health orgs under-cover.

Skipped:
- rank 1 (Reddit r/selfimprovement thread — covered by the dedicated Stage 1.5a Reddit fetch, no need to double-fetch as SERP)
- rank 5 (duplicate of rank 2 — same Mental Health Foundation article, different URL fragment)
