## Industry-take themes

- **Theme:** People want to quit the *behavior* without quitting the *platform* (no deactivation).
  - Evidence: "how do i stop doomscrolling without having to deactivate," @nothingneuw, source: https://x.com/nothingneuw/status/2078193206475575519
  - (Reinforces the SERP's dominant "without quitting your phone / without going off the grid" framing.)

- **Theme:** Doomscrolling evades the social shame that normally curbs bad habits — everyone does it, so no one has standing to police it.
  - Evidence: "no one has the moral authority to police doomscrollers. Parents cannot tell kids to stop doomscrolling because they can't stop doomscrolling," @yenceesanjeev, source: https://x.com/yenceesanjeev/status/2076939645699661856

- **Theme:** "It's useful, though" is the self-justification that keeps people hooked.
  - Evidence: "Add to it the pretext of 'I'm only using it for useful things'… thinly veiled promotional content masked as learning content," @yenceesanjeev, source: https://x.com/yenceesanjeev/status/2076939645699661856
