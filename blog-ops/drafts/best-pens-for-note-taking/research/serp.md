## Avoid pitfalls

Things the SERP shows are already commoditized or done-to-death. Don't repeat.

- Generic "handwriting boosts memory" open (used near-verbatim by both JetPens and Pilot AU, citing the same or similar cognitive-science hook) — don't lead with this; if used, keep it brief and cite properly rather than repeating it as the whole intro.
- Undifferentiated "gel pens are smooth but smudge, ballpoints are reliable but need pressure" mechanics explainer with no product tie-in (Bastion's entire approach) — the SERP shows this angle alone, without concrete picks, doesn't win; must be paired with actual recommendations.
- Padding a "pens" list with highlighters and permanent markers (Small Stuff Counts) — the brief's own target of "12 picks, don't pad" argues against this; keep scope to actual note-taking pens.
