# Product

## Identity

Olga's free AI tools — a small suite of AI-powered productivity tools built
on OpenAI that automate quick writing and comprehension tasks, so readers
spend less time on the mundane and more on what matters.

## Features

- **Email Generator:** drafts an email from a short prompt so you never start
  from a blank page.
- **Text Simplifier:** rewrites dense or complex text into plain,
  easy-to-read language.
- **Acronym Explainer:** expands and explains unfamiliar acronyms in context.
- **Text Summarizer:** condenses long text into a short, scannable summary.

## Differentiators

- Free to use, no signup friction
- Single-purpose tools that each do one job well (not a bloated all-in-one)
- Built and personally used by Olga, powered by OpenAI
- Made for everyday students and professionals, not enterprise teams
