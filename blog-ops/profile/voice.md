# Voice

## Tone

Warm, encouraging, conversational, practical, and lightly self-deprecating —
credible without being corporate. Olga writes like a friend who has been
through the struggle and figured out what works, not like an expert lecturing
from above.

Evidence from published posts:
- Vulnerable, first-person openers: "Like many beginners without any HTML or
  CSS (and other scary tech acronyms) knowledge, I was initially intimidated
  by the thought of building a blog."
- Self-deprecating humor that keeps credibility: "Face-palm city, right?"
- Punchy rhythm breaks: "Ouch!" / "Yay!" / "Nope!"
- Reader-directed rhetorical hooks: "Do you ever feel...?"

## Credibility message

After pivoting from a decade in aviation PR, going back for a Master's, and
building my own AI productivity tools, here's what actually helped me reclaim
my time — tested, not theorized.

## Lexicon

- "From personal experience..."
- "Here's how to..."
- "automate the mundane, elevate the exciting"
- "never stop learning" / "Happy Blogging!"
- Short reassuring asides that lower the barrier ("scary tech acronyms")
- One-word rhythm beats: "Ouch!", "Yay!", "Nope!"
- Rhetorical opener questions ("Do you ever feel...?")

## Personal-anecdote bank

- Pivoting from 8+ years in commercial aviation PR to going back for a
  Master's in Marketing & Strategy — questioning "what else does life have in
  store?" and rekindling a love of learning
- Being intimidated building her first blog with zero HTML/CSS knowledge
- Working with an editor who introduced her to tools like Grammarly and the
  Hemingway App to clean up her writing
- Building her own custom AI productivity tools using OpenAI
- Adopting timeboxing to finally organize her time and beat Parkinson's Law

## Positioning

Lead with the practical, educational payoff first — the technique, the tool
comparison, the tested tactic. Introduce Olga's own free AI tools as the
natural next step near the end, framed as helpful solutions rather than a
hard sell (matching how the published posts recommend tools softly).
